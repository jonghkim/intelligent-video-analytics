# Intelligent Video Analytics with Deep Learning

This repository contains teaching materials for the guest lecture
**"Intelligent Video Analytics with Deep Learning"**, delivered in the
KAIST MBA course *Business Analytics* (IM561) (Prof. Wonseok Oh).

---

## Teaching Summary

- **Instructor:** Jongho Kim (Ph.D. Candidate, Cornell University)
- **Audience:** MBA students (KAIST)
- **Level:** Applied machine learning / business analytics

This lecture introduces the foundations of video analytics and deep learning
for social science research and business applications, with a focus on:

1. **Video Analytics and Social Science Research**
   - Applications of video data in empirical research
   - Measuring human behavior from visual signals

2. **Underlying Mechanisms of Deep Learning**
   - Neural network architectures for visual data
   - Convolutional neural networks (CNN)

3. **Detecting Labels in Your Own Video Data**
   - Practical pipeline for video feature extraction
   - Hands-on implementation with real data

The materials emphasize a **data-driven and empirical approach** to
video analytics, connecting deep learning methods with real-world
research and business applications.
